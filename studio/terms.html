1. Packages and what’s included

We offer two packages, described on our packages page:

  • Essential (R900): our standard design built from your questionnaire, with one session with your developer.
  • Bespoke (R1 999): an in-depth questionnaire, three sessions with your developer and the Bespoke-only features listed on the packages page.

Both packages include your own web address and hosting at our cost for the period in section 7. Prices are once-off, in South African rand, and are fixed at the price shown when you order. [If you are VAT-registered: “Prices include VAT.” If not: “We are not VAT-registered.”]

Anything not listed in your package (for example extra pages on Essential, or new features after launch) is extra. We’ll always quote and get your OK before doing paid work.

2. Payment

  • A 50% deposit books your spot, and we start once it’s paid.
  • The balance is due when you approve your website, before it goes live on your web address.
  • We send payment details or a secure payment link with your confirmation. We never ask for card details by email or WhatsApp.
  • Extra sessions (R350 each) and other agreed extras are paid before they take place.

3. Timeline

Essential websites are usually ready for your session within 7 days, and Bespoke websites within 3–4 weeks. These times start once we have your deposit, your completed questionnaire and your photos. Waiting on information or on a session date moves the timeline along with it.

Tell us early if your invitations go out on a particular date, and we’ll do our best to have your site live in time.

4. Sessions with your developer

  • Essential includes one session of about 45 minutes; Bespoke includes three of about an hour each. Sessions are by video call, or in person where we agree to it.
  • Please book your sessions within 3 months of your order. Unused sessions lapse after that, or once your site has launched, whichever comes first.
  • You can reschedule for free with at least 24 hours’ notice. A session missed without notice counts as used.
  • Changes you ask for during a session are included. Ideas that need a lot of new work (for example a whole new page on Essential) may be quoted separately.

5. Changes after launch

For 14 days after launch, we fix any mistakes and make small factual updates (a time, a spelling, a parking note) for free. After that, or for bigger changes, we’ll quote before doing the work. If your wedding is postponed, we update the date and details once for free.

  • You’re responsible for checking that the information on your site is correct before you approve it.
  • Your website is public to anyone with the link. Please don’t ask us to publish ID numbers, home addresses or anything you’d rather keep private. The optional gallery question keeps casual visitors out, but it is not a high-security lock.
  • Your content stays yours. You give us permission to use it only to build and run your website.
  • Portfolio: we only show your website or photos in our examples with your permission, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • We may decline content that is unlawful, hateful or infringes someone else’s rights.

7. Web address and hosting

  • We register your web address (for example a .co.za) and host your website at our cost, subject to the address being available.
  • Your website stays online until 3 months after your wedding date for Essential, or 12 months for Bespoke. We’ll remind you before it goes offline.
  • If you’d like to keep it online longer, we can quote for an extension. If you’d like to keep the web address itself, we’ll transfer it to you on request; any registrar fees after the included period are yours.
  • We aim to keep your website available at all times, but can’t guarantee uninterrupted service from hosting and internet providers.

8. Cancellations and refunds
