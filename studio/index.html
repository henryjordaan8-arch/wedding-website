Questions

Good to know

Is the price really once-off?

Yes. You pay once for the build and hosting for the period in your package. The only extra is your own domain name (e.g. janandpieter.co.za) if you want one, which is usually a small yearly fee paid directly to the domain registrar — we help you set it up.

How do RSVPs work?

Guests fill in a simple form on your website. Every answer goes straight into a spreadsheet you can open on your phone — names, meal choices, song requests, whatever you’d like to ask.

Can I change things after it’s live?

Yes. Essential includes a session with your developer to customise your website once the first version is ready. Bespoke includes unlimited tweaks during your three sessions. Small updates after that (like a new time or a parking note) are quick and affordable.

What’s the difference between Essential and Bespoke?

Essential uses our proven design with your choice of 15 palettes, 8 lettering styles and three opening animations, plus one session with your developer — quick, beautiful and affordable. Bespoke adds three sessions, your exact wedding colours, the doors and curtain openings, both languages, up to 40 photos, extra pages and special touches, and a year online after the wedding — for only R700 more.

Can the website be in Afrikaans?

Absolutely. Essential websites are in English or Afrikaans; Bespoke websites can have both side by side.

How far before the wedding should we order?

Ideally when you send save-the-dates or invitations, so guests can RSVP online. Essential takes about a week; Bespoke about three to four weeks depending on when the sessions are booked.

How do I pay?

A 50% deposit secures your spot, and the balance is paid when you approve your website before it goes live.
